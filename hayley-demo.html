    🎤 Microphone is blocked — here's how to turn it on
    This demo's two-way call needs the mic. If you blocked it by accident, fix it in under a minute:
    1. Open this demo in the Chrome app — not inside another app's built-in browser (like a chat or social app). If you're inside another app, tap its ⋮ menu and choose “Open in Chrome”.
    2. Tap the lock icon (or the tune/settings icon) next to the address bar at the top of Chrome.
    3. Tap Site settings, then set Microphone to Allow.
    4. Come back here, reload the page, then tap Start Call again.
